# SPRUT GSAP creative effects

## Visual identity

- Canvas: transparent overlay over approved footage; use `#070707` only for a localized plate.
- Accent: `#FF6A00`, reserved for the active word, route, state change, or payoff.
- Primary: `#FFFFFF`; secondary: `#A8A8A8`; panel: `#121212`.
- Typography: bundled Unbounded for one expressive idea, Golos Text for readable copy, JetBrains Mono for data.
- Motion follows meaning: split type for emphasis, morphing for transformation, a drawn route for sequence, scramble for a data reveal, and Flip for a before/after rearrangement.

## Motion rules

- Build the readable final layout first, then animate into it.
- First movement starts after 0.1 seconds. Entrances use ease-out, moves use ease-in-out, and the final exit is shorter than the entrance.
- One hero effect per approved visual. No infinite repeat, random values, asynchronous timeline construction, network request, or media playback.
- The exact approved words are the only semantic text. Decorative geometry may support them but may not introduce a new claim.

## What not to do

- Do not add blue, cyan, violet, or green accents.
- Do not replace an original white diagram with an orange one.
- Do not use a plugin because it is available; the selected effect must match the approved purpose.
- Do not hide a weak edit with an effect.
- Do not render or publish from the scaffolder.
